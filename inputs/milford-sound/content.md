# Milford Sound cruises

## Hero

Get spectacular views of the Tasman Sea and Mitre Peak. Sail the 16km fiord, with commentary as you pass the waterfalls and Seal Rock.

## FAQs

### Can I cancel?

Most of the cruise tickets that were opened allow a full refund up to 24 hours before departure. Check the product you pick.

### How do I get there?

Cruises leave from the Milford Sound terminal. Coach tours from Queenstown or Te Anau include the ride. A cruise-only ticket does not.

### Is there wheelchair access?

The collection FAQ says many boats have step-free boarding and an accessible main deck. Upper decks may have stairs. Say so when you book.
